I am a postdoctoral researcher at the [Department of Communication and Arts](https://www.ruc.dk/eng/communication/), Roskilde University. I work in the intersection between politics, media and digital society. Currently, I investigate the role of anti-systemic narratives and far-right and counter-climate movements in digital information environments in Northern and Central Europe.

I am a postdoc in two research projects: [Alternative Media and Ideological Counterpublics (AlterPublics)](https://www.ruc.dk/eng/research/alternative-media-and-ideological-counterpublics/) (PI Eva Mayerhöffer, funded by the Carlsberg Foundation; 2021–2025) and [Modelling Polarization Dynamics on Danish Social Media (#echopol)](https://www.ruc.dk/eng/research/modelling-polarization-dynamics-on-danish-social-media-echopol/) (co-lead with Eva Mayerhöffer and Jens Ulrik Hansen, funded by Independent Research Fund Denmark; 2023–2026).

My Ph.D. dissertation, [BURN IT DOWN: Anti-Systemic Narratives and Counterpublics in Digital Alternative Information Environments](https://forskning.ruc.dk/en/publications/burn-it-down-anti-systemic-narratives-and-counterpublics-in-digit/), studied the spread of anti-systemic narratives and discourses in alternative information environments across four European countries between 2019 and 2022 — the societal information space outside the mainstream, related to the spread of disinformation, online extremism, and extra-parliamentary politics.
